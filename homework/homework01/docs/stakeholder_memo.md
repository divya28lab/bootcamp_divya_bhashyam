# Stakeholder Memo — Market Early-Warning System

## Primary Stakeholder
**Portfolio Manager / Risk Manager**

## Decision Need
The stakeholder needs to identify periods when market conditions are becoming unusually stressed so that they can investigate portfolio risk and consider further risk analysis.

## User
Financial analysts and investment professionals who monitor market conditions and interpret the system's warning signals.

## Expected Output
A market stress score and an early-warning signal indicating whether current market conditions show signs of elevated risk.

## Timing
The system will be updated as new market data becomes available and used for periodic market-risk monitoring.

## Key Decision
When the system indicates elevated market stress, the stakeholder can investigate the underlying indicators and perform additional risk or stress analysis.
